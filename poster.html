The Problem
Legal services in Kazakhstan are inaccessible to most people.

73% of citizens have never used a professional legal service — not because they don't need one, but because the system is too slow, too expensive, and too opaque.

73%
No Access to Legal Help
Nearly three-quarters of Kazakhstanis cannot afford or access qualified legal assistance when they need it most — leaving contracts unsigned and disputes unresolved.
1–2wk
Average Processing Time
A standard legal document takes one to two weeks to prepare, review, and finalize through traditional channels — far too slow for modern business needs.
1 in 4
Documents Contain Critical Errors
Manual document preparation leads to costly mistakes that invalidate contracts — often discovered only when it's too late to correct them.

Platform Features
Everything automated. Nothing left to chance.
AI Risk Analysis
AI scans every document for legal risks, ambiguities, and compliance issues — delivering a full report in seconds.
Smart Templates
Lawyer-verified templates for contracts and deeds — with dynamic field validation and guided input.
Rule Engine
Lawyers configure validation rules, risk triggers, and conditional logic. Every document auto-checks all rules.
Lawyer Matching
ML-powered matching connects users to the right specialist based on document type and lawyer expertise.
Version History
Full revision tracking with one-click restore to any version and a complete timestamped audit trail.
Secure Storage
Encrypted MinIO S3 storage with signed download URLs, PDF export, and revocable secure share links.
Clause Explainer
AI translates complex legal clauses into plain language so users fully understand every term they agree to.
Audit & Analytics
Complete action logs, admin dashboards, and platform analytics for full compliance oversight.
